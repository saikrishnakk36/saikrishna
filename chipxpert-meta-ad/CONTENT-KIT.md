# ChipXpert — Meta Ads Video Campaign Kit

Prepared for Sai Krishna (Founder, ChipXpert). Everything below is decision-ready:
the strategy, the exact scripts, the shot list for the presenter version, the
campaign build, lead handling, a funnel model with numbers, and the claims/policy
checklist. The rendered motion-graphic ad in `output/` uses Script A verbatim.

---

## 0. The strategy in one paragraph

We are not selling "a VLSI course". We are selling the escape from a non-core job
to an ECE/EEE graduate who is scared of ending up in IT support or a service
company. The hook names that fear in 2 seconds, the middle stacks credibility
that no local competitor can match in one breath (Cadence Academic Partner,
AICTE, NSDC, ex-Intel trainers, 500+ alumni, a live design-services company
behind the institute), and the CTA is low-friction: a free demo class. Lead
quality beats lead volume for a ₹40k–₹1L decision, so the lead form carries
two qualifying questions and every lead gets a call within 5 minutes.

## 2. Video length: what to make

| Cut | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **20–22 s** | Cold traffic, Reels + Stories + Feed. Hook 0–3 s, problem 3–6 s, proof 6–15 s, offer + CTA 15–22 s. |
| Short | 15 s | A/B against the primary on cold traffic. |
| Bumper | 6 s | Retargeting: logo + one proof line + CTA. |
| Long | 35–45 s | Retargeting people who watched 50%+ of the primary: "a day in the ChipXpert lab". |

Why not 15 s only: Meta's 15-second guidance is tuned for brand recall. A
₹40k–₹1L course needs 8–10 seconds of proof before the ask, and lead-gen video
ads for education consistently hold up at 20–25 s when the hook lands in the
first 2 seconds. Build 22 s and 15 s, run both for 5 days, keep the winner.

## 4. Script B — presenter version with a college girl (UGC style, 22 s)

This is the version to shoot. UGC-style presenter ads out-perform pure motion
graphics on Reels for education because the viewer sees "someone like me". The
rendered end card (`output/chipxpert_endcard_9x16.mp4`) is built to be appended
to this footage so the brand close is consistent across both versions.

### Casting notes

- Female, 20–25, reads as a final-year student or a fresh graduate. Ideally an
  actual ChipXpert student or alumna, or an ECE student from JNTU, CBIT, VNR, MGIT,
  GRIET, BVRIT, Osmania, or a Bengaluru college. "Attractive" here means
  well-lit, confident, natural, energetic and relatable. Meta viewers trust
  "someone like me", not a model, and Meta rejects anything sexualised anyway.
- Natural makeup, college casual (kurti or tee with jeans). ChipXpert lanyard or
  ID card in the lab scenes.
- Clear English with a natural Telugu or Kannada inflection is a plus for local
  trust. Fast, conversational delivery. No reading voice.
- Pay ₹3k–₹8k for a half-day. Get a signed release covering paid social usage for
  12 months. If she is a real alumna, use Script B2 (testimonial). If she is an
  actor or a current student, use Script B1 (host). Never let an actor claim to
  be an alumna. That is a Meta and ASCI violation and it will get found out.

### Locations and gear

1. College corridor, hostel room, or a café near campus (hook shot).
2. ChipXpert Madhapur lab: Cadence Innovus or Virtuoso layouts visible on monitors.
3. Whiteboard with a floorplan or clock-tree sketch (B-roll).

Phone camera (iPhone 13 or newer, or a Pixel), 4K 30 fps, vertical. One ring
light plus window light. A ₹1.5k lavalier mic or wired earphone mic. Presenter at
arm's length, eyes on the lens. Three takes of every line. Jump-cut every 2–3 s
in the edit. Burn in captions: 80%+ of Reels are watched muted.

## 6. Campaign build

- **Objective:** Leads.
- **Conversion location:** Run two ad sets. Ad set 1: Instant Forms (volume). Ad set 2: WhatsApp click-to-chat (quality; your counsellor replies in-app).
- **Budget:** ₹1,500/day total to start (₹1,000 Instant Form, ₹500 WhatsApp). Advantage campaign budget on. Scale winners 20% per day, never more, or the learning phase resets.
- **Bid strategy:** Highest volume for 7 days. Switch to cost-per-result goal at 1.2× your observed CPL once you have 50+ leads.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements, then remove Audience Network and In-stream. Keep Reels, Stories, Feed, Explore.
- **Creative:** 9:16 primary (1080×1920). Let Meta crop to 4:5 for Feed; the rendered video keeps all text inside the centre safe zone so the crop is clean. Upload the 4:5 render separately if Feed CPL is more than 30% worse.
- **Creative per ad set:** 3 videos (Script A default hook, Script A hook 3, Script B once shot). Meta needs 3+ creatives to optimise.
- **Schedule:** Always on. Ads shown 7 am–11 pm IST only (students scroll late, so do not cut before 11).
- **Retargeting ad set (₹300/day):** video viewers 50%+ (30 days), Instant Form openers who did not submit, Instagram engagers (60 days). Show the 6 s bumper and the 35–45 s lab video.

## 8. Lead handling (this is where enrolments are won or lost)

Speed to lead is the single biggest lever. Leads called within 5 minutes convert
several times better than leads called after an hour.

| Touch | When | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | T+5 min | Call | "Hi [name], this is [counsellor] from ChipXpert. You just booked a free VLSI demo. Are you looking at Physical Design, Design Verification, or not sure yet?" |
| 2 | T+1 h | WhatsApp | Demo slot options (Sat 11 am / Sun 11 am), map pin, a 30-second lab video |
| 3 | T+1 day | WhatsApp | One alumni story (name, batch, company, LinkedIn link) |
| 4 | T+3 days | Call | Answer objections: fees, EMI, duration, placements |
| 5 | T+6 days | WhatsApp | Demo reminder plus "seats left in this batch: N" |
| 6 | T+14 days | WhatsApp | Scholarship test or early-bird deadline |

WhatsApp auto-reply for the click-to-chat ad set:
```
Hi 👋 Thanks for reaching out to ChipXpert.
Quick question so we send you the right details:
1️⃣ Physical Design
2️⃣ Design Verification
3️⃣ Not sure yet, tell me more
Reply with a number.
```

## 9. Funnel model (assumptions to validate in the first 14 days)

| Stage | Assumption | Monthly at ₹45k spend |
|---|---|---|
| Spend | ₹1,500/day | ₹45,000 |
| Cost per lead (Instant Form) | ₹150 (expect ₹90–₹300) | 300 leads |
| Qualified (ECE/EEE/EIE, 2023–2026, HYD/BLR or online) | 35% | 105 |
| Demo attended | 35% of qualified | 37 |
| Enrolled | 12% of demo attendees | 4–5 |
| Course fee | ₹60,000 (replace with actual) | ₹2.4–3.0 L revenue |
| Cost per enrolment | | ≈ ₹10,000 |

If CPL lands at ₹300 and the demo rate halves, the same spend yields about one
enrolment. In that case the ad is not the problem; the 5-minute callback and the
demo show-up rate are. Fix those before touching creative. Targets to hold: CPL
under ₹200, qualified rate over 30%, demo show-up over 30%.

## 10. First 14 days

- Day 0: Launch 2 ad sets × 3 creatives. Install Meta Pixel + Conversions API on the website, and connect the Instant Form to the CRM or a Google Sheet with an instant WhatsApp/SMS to the counsellor.
- Day 3: Kill any creative with CPL over 2× the best creative after ₹3,000 spend. Do not touch anything else.
- Day 5: Compare 22 s vs 15 s cut. Keep the winner, replace the loser with a new hook.
- Day 7: Check the qualified rate per ad set. If Instant Form qualified rate is under 25%, switch the form to Higher intent if it is not already, and add "Graduation year" as the first question.
- Day 10: Upload alumni list, build the 1% lookalike, launch it as a third ad set at ₹500/day.
- Day 14: Scale the best ad set 20%/day while CPL holds. Shoot Script B if not done yet; it will be the next winner.

Metrics to look at, in order: cost per qualified lead, demo show-up rate, hook rate
(3-second views ÷ impressions; aim over 30%), hold rate (ThruPlay ÷ 3-second
views; aim over 35%), CPL last.

## 11. Claims and policy checklist

Every claim in the ad must be true and provable on request. Meta's education
policy and India's ASCI rules both apply.

| Claim used | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| AICTE-approved | Use | Keep the approval letter handy |
| NSDC-certified | Use | Same |
| Cadence Academic Partner | Use | Same |
| 500+ alumni, 17+ batches | Use | Keep the count current |
| Ex-Intel trainers / "done this at Intel" | Use only if at least one active trainer has Intel experience | Founder-level Intel experience counts if the founder teaches or mentors the batch |
| Placement assistance | Use | Never "100% placement" or "guaranteed job" |
| Salary figures | Avoid unless you can cite a specific alumna with consent | |
| "India needs thousands of VLSI engineers" | Soften to "India is building its own chips now" unless you cite a source on screen | |

Wording rules: ask questions ("Still stuck in a non-core job?"), do not assert
attributes about the viewer ("You are unemployed"). No before/after salary claims.
No competitor names. Keep the presenter's clothing and framing plain. Testimonials
only from real students with a signed consent.
